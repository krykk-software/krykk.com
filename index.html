About Krykk

Krykk Ltd is led by Raphael Vergnaud, a revenue leader with more than 25 years of enterprise software sales experience across account executive and CRO roles.

His experience includes technology companies such as Tangoe, Zendrive and DiffusionData, working with enterprise buyers across the UK and Europe. Krykk brings that experience to founders and leadership teams who want to grow faster with fewer missteps.

Krykk also builds its own software, so the advice comes from people who ship products as well as sell them.
